# Demo clear: credits

The route, blocks, spike, warp and clear block are original demo art made for IWBTG Collection.

The Kid sprites (`sprites/kid_idle.png`, `kid_run.png`, `kid_jump.png`, `kid_fall.png`) and the bullet (`sprites/kid_bullet.png`) come from **I wanna Little Trap** by **waka** (https://delicious-fruit.com/game/21365), taken from its conversion to our package format. The Kid is the character and sprite of **I Wanna Be The Guy** by **Kayin**, as shipped in the IWBTG fangame engine. The kid physics are the A1 engine family preset, the same as Little Trap's.

These sprites are game content, not covered by the repository's code or documentation licenses. They are here, few and credited, by the user's decision of 2026-11-11 (nugamus/iwbtg-collection-games#34). Their owners can have them removed.
