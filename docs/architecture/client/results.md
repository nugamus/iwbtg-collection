# The client: Results

Part of [the client](../client.md).

The results screen (`results/`, #41; docs/product.md, Results screen) is one page after a cleared run, built from the artboards `Results169`, `Results169Slower`, `Results43` and `Results43Slower` (docs/design/mockups/m4) with the [UI](ui.md) kit.

- **When.** A clear that goes on a [stats](stats.md) board opens it: `stats` keeps the run with what it changed (`Stats::cleared` with `Stats::last`) and `results::open` shows each clear once. Only runs started as a new game go on a board, so a clear from a save (Play original resuming the save file) gets no results page; quit or abandoned runs never clear.
- **While it shows.** The sim does not tick (`SimTick` is gated, as the pause menu gates it), the game camera is off, the HUD is suspended (`hud::Suspended`: columns and the game's shadow hidden, the UI scale left to the results), the backdrop takes the results dim (`dim::RESULTS`), and Escape does not open the pause menu. The page draws on a camera of its own (order 20, UI only), laid out at its artboard's size (1920×1080 at 16:10 and wider, 1024×768 below: the player card's `Variant::for_results` rule) and scaled to fit the window, wider windows giving the columns more room.
- **Data.** `Summary::new` reads the run, its `Recorded` and the board: the placement among your runs, the PB before and the delta, per segment its length, split, delta against the PB's splits (none on a first clear) and gold, the sum of best and the possible time save (the run minus the sum of best), deaths per segment, the other runs for the leaderboard, and the run's death log (`Run::death_marks`: each death's time, segment, room and position; [stats](stats.md)) as `Summary::death_log`. Segments are named `Stage 1` ... (`Run` for a game without stages); the bars show the number.
- **Shown.** Header (the package's title, the map when there are several, Ranked or Play original, the mods; `Cleared 3 Nov 2026, 21:14` in the player's time zone like the leaderboard's dates, `Summary::cleared_at(offset)`). Left: the [live leaderboard](hud.md) frozen at this run's place (`Live::frozen`: #1 pinned over three dots when out of view, your row `This run` with its final time). Hero: the time with the hundredths smaller; the `New PB` chip with the delicious fruit (the pause menu's: the package's `cherry`, else our placeholder) or `Not a PB`; the placement `#3 Local`, or the Unranked pill for an unranked run. Rank change: `Local placement` (`#3`, `of 10 runs`; ranked only) and `Personal best` (`39:40.12 → 38:12.46` with the green delta, `38:12.46 stands` with the red one, or `First clear`); the `→` is text, from the Noto Sans Math fallback ([UI kit](ui.md)). Right: the [player card](card.md), `results` or `results-compact`. Splits table (stage, segment, split, vs PB; gold `★` segments; sum of best, possible time save, the legend; rows past the room are cut). Deaths per stage as bars, the stage with the most deaths brightest. Under them the death timeline (`Summary::timeline`): `0:00 → <time>` on the right, a base line, the stage boundaries (the splits but the last) as taller, 2 px marks in `BAR_PEAK`, and the deaths as ticks in `BAR`. Deaths are gathered into 480 slots along the run (`TIMELINE_SLOTS`), one tick per slot with deaths, 0.3 of the line's height for one death up to 0.8 for the busiest slot, so thousands of deaths still draw at most 480 nodes. It is 56 px high in the wide layout and 40 px in the compact one (`tlH`). A run without a log (one from before #679) shows the line and the boundaries only.
- **Heatmap** (#654; the `[results] heatmap` setting, off by default, or `--results-heatmap true|false`, which is saved like the other setting flags; the wide layout only, as on the artboard). Under the timeline: the stage's room, dimmed, with soft red dots where you died, and `Where you died · Stage 5` with how many deaths it shows. The stage is the splits row under the pointer, else the last one clicked, else the stage with the most deaths. `Summary::heat` picks the stage's room with the most deaths that have a known spot and gathers them into 32 px cells (`HEAT_CELL`), a dot per cell at the mean position of its deaths, sized and coloured by its count (the busiest 48 cells). The room is drawn by `results/room.rs` into an image of the room's size: the room as it starts (colour, background layers, tiles from `art::still`, and the instances placed in the room file at their first frame through `view::place_sprite`), on a render layer and a camera of its own, so the game camera never sees it; instances the room creates while it runs are not in it. Each room is drawn once while the page shows and dropped when it closes. `results::heat` fills the panel again only when the shown stage changes.
- **Placement is Local** until the Global scope exists (#484): your PB is always #1 among your own runs, so the hero shows this run's own place instead of the PB's (the artboard's `Not a PB · PB #25` reads `Not a PB · #3 Local`).
- **Footer.** Back (or Escape) closes the page and returns to the game until game select exists (#148); keys pressed on the page are dropped. Retry (click, or hold R for 0.5 s; the button shows pressed while R is held) asks `retry.rs` for a quick-retry, which closes the page (`results::reset`). Save replay copies the run's replay to `exports/<game id>-<name>.iwbr` in the data area, kept whatever replay pruning does, and then reads `Replay saved`; it is off when the run has no replay (a `--screenshot` check keeps none). Watch replay waits for the replay viewer (#42), Upload replay for M7; both show faded and do nothing.
- **Preview.** `iwbtg-client <package> --ranked --results-preview <pb|slower> [--window WxH] [--screenshot <png>]` (hidden from `--help`) opens the page at once with the artboard's sample run: the board of the leaderboard sample's runs takes the Kaenbyou run (a new PB at 38:12.46, or 39:58.40 behind it) through the same `Board::add` and `Summary::new` as a real clear, with the stage names `Stage 1` to `Boss`. Its death log is the artboard's timeline (each death at a time from the artboard script's generator and seed in its stage) with the artboard's red spots placed in the room the package starts in, so the heatmap draws a real room of the loaded package. A `--screenshot` check starts from the default settings, so add `--results-heatmap true` to see the heatmap.
