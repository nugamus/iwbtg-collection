# The client: Results

Part of [the client](../client.md).

The results screen (`results/`, #41; docs/product.md, Results screen) is one page after a cleared run, built from the artboards `Results169`, `Results169Slower`, `Results43` and `Results43Slower` (docs/design/mockups/m4) with the [UI](ui.md) kit.

- **When.** A clear that goes on a [stats](stats.md) board opens it: `stats` keeps the run with what it changed (`Stats::cleared` with `Stats::last`) and `results::open` shows each clear once. Only runs started as a new game go on a board, so a clear from a save (Play original resuming the save file) gets no results page; quit or abandoned runs never clear.
- **While it shows.** The sim does not tick (`SimTick` is gated, as the pause menu gates it), the game camera is off, the HUD is suspended (`hud::Suspended`: columns and the game's shadow hidden, the UI scale left to the results), the backdrop takes the results dim (`dim::RESULTS`), and Escape does not open the pause menu. The page draws on a camera of its own (order 20, UI only), laid out at its artboard's size (1920×1080 at 16:10 and wider, 1024×768 below: the player card's `Variant::for_results` rule) and scaled to fit the window, wider windows giving the columns more room.
- **Data.** `Summary::new` reads the run, its `Recorded` and the board: the placement among your runs, the PB before and the delta, per segment its length, split, delta against the PB's splits (none on a first clear) and gold, the sum of best and the possible time save (the run minus the sum of best), deaths per segment, and the other runs for the leaderboard. Segments are named `Stage 1` ... (`Run` for a game without stages); the bars show the number.
- **Shown.** Header (the package's title, the map when there are several, Ranked or Play original, the mods; `Cleared 3 Nov 2026, 21:14` in UTC like the leaderboard's dates). Left: the [live leaderboard](hud.md) frozen at this run's place (`Live::frozen`: #1 pinned over three dots when out of view, your row `This run` with its final time). Hero: the time with the hundredths smaller; the `New PB` chip with the delicious fruit (the pause menu's: the package's `cherry`, else our placeholder) or `Not a PB`; the placement `#3 Local`, or the Unranked pill for an unranked run. Rank change: `Local placement` (`#3`, `of 10 runs`; ranked only) and `Personal best` (`39:40.12 → 38:12.46` with the green delta, `38:12.46 stands` with the red one, or `First clear`); the arrow is the kit's `Icon::To` since no bundled font has `→`. Right: the [player card](card.md), `results` or `results-compact`. Splits table (stage, segment, split, vs PB; gold `★` segments; sum of best, possible time save, the legend; rows past the room are cut). Deaths per stage as bars, the stage with the most deaths brightest.
- **Placement is Local** until the Global scope exists (#484): your PB is always #1 among your own runs, so the hero shows this run's own place instead of the PB's (the artboard's `Not a PB · PB #25` reads `Not a PB · #3 Local`).
- **Footer.** Back (or Escape) closes the page and returns to the game until game select exists (#148); keys pressed on the page are dropped. Retry (click, or hold R for 0.5 s; the button shows pressed while R is held) asks `retry.rs` for a quick-retry, which closes the page (`results::reset`). Save replay copies the run's replay to `exports/<game id>-<name>.iwbr` in the data area, kept whatever replay pruning does, and then reads `Replay saved`; it is off when the run has no replay (a `--screenshot` check keeps none). Watch replay waits for the replay viewer (#42), Upload replay for M7; both show faded and do nothing.
- **Not yet.** The death timeline and the per-stage death heatmap need each death's time and place, which stats do not keep yet (#654).
- **Preview.** `iwbtg-client <package> --ranked --results-preview <pb|slower> [--window WxH] [--screenshot <png>]` (hidden from `--help`) opens the page at once with the artboard's sample run: the board of the leaderboard sample's runs takes the Kaenbyou run (a new PB at 38:12.46, or 39:58.40 behind it) through the same `Board::add` and `Summary::new` as a real clear, with the stage names `Stage 1` to `Boss`.
