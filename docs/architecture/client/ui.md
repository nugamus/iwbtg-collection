# The client: UI

Part of [the client](../client.md).

The client's screens (game select, mod select, loading, HUD, pause, results) are built with bevy_ui and the kit in `crates/client/src/ui/` ([0003 UI toolkit](../../decisions/0003-ui-toolkit.md) has the options weighed). Screens are built from the M4 mockups (`docs/design/mockups/m4/`); every colour, size and shape comes from the kit, never from the screen.

- `tokens.rs`: the mockups' colours by name (ground, ink, muted, soft, accent, ahead, behind, gold, amber, ranked, the glass fills, the leaderboard rows and the player card's glass and its solid colour, avatar gradient and progress bar), the star ramp (`star_color`: the last step at or below the rating), shear 0.2, radius 10 px and the motion durations (the leaderboard's 450 ms reorder and 3 s pace hold among them). Artboard alphas (#627): the mockups' CSS blends `rgba(...)` in sRGB, Bevy blends in linear light, so an alpha copied from the CSS covers less (black at 0.5 leaves about 73 % of what is under it, not 50 %). Any translucent colour a screen takes from an artboard goes through the kit: `tokens::black(a)` for shadows and dims (`1 - (1 - a)^2.2`, exact for black over anything), `tokens::dark(colour, a)` for near-black glass (the same rule; the `CARD` constants are it precomputed), `tokens::over(colour, a, ground)` for a light or coloured overlay (the alpha that gives the browser's luminance over that ground, `GROUND` for the dark screens; only approximate over other grounds), or an opaque colour mixed in sRGB by hand when the ground is fixed (`TRACK`). A shape's own layers (veil over its image, band, ring) are mixed in sRGB inside `shape.wgsl` and take the CSS alphas as they are; only what blends with what is behind the node (fill, shadow) needs converting. A blurred shadow converts its peak alpha; the falloff stays the shader's.
- `fonts.rs`: the bundled fonts, compiled into the binary (Barlow, Barlow Condensed, Pixelify Sans for the pause box only, a Noto Sans Symbols 2 subset as the fallback for `★ ▾ ✓` and arrows, and a Noto Sans JP subset as the Japanese fallback; all SIL OFL 1.1, licences next to the files in `crates/client/assets/fonts/`). `Type` is a text style (family, size, weight, italic, tracking in em, capitals) with the mockups' styles as constants (`LABEL`, `STAT`, `BODY`, `BUTTON`, `PRIMARY`, `STARS`, `PILL_CAPS`, `MOD_CODE`, `TITLE`). Every style turns on tabular numerals.
- `shape.rs`, `shape.wgsl`: `Shape` on a node draws it sheared (0.2) with rounded corners while its children stay upright. A shape has a fill and optionally an image (upright, covering the shape, clipped to it), a three-stop veil gradient, a band along the left or bottom edge, an outer ring and a drop shadow. A `ShapeLayer` child, grown past the node's box by the overhang, ring and shadow, draws it with `ShapeMaterial`; `sync_shapes` (PostUpdate, after layout) adds the layer and rewrites the material only when something changed. `UiOpacity` multiplies opacity down the tree; `FadeMask { top, bottom }` fades a list over its top and bottom edges (shapes per pixel, text per line); text spawned through the kit carries its colour in `Ink` so both can apply, and an icon given an `Ink` (its tint) fades the same way. Stacking is plain bevy_ui: a shape's layer draws right after its node and before its children, so a shape in a higher `GlobalZIndex` covers everything below it. The glass fills (`CARD`, `CARD_MID`, `CARD_WARM`) are see-through by design, and because Bevy blends in linear light even `CARD`'s 0.6 % shows white text under it; a shape that floats over other UI uses a solid fill (`CARD_SOLID`, #664).
- `widgets.rs`: `text`, `rich` (one line in several styles and colours, as spans; spans do not fade), `sheared`, `panel`, `pill`, `star_pill`, `mod_pill`, `ranked_pill`, `unranked_pill`, `tag_pill`, `stat` and `button` (Primary, Secondary, Control), each a function returning a bundle. Buttons are `bevy_ui_widgets::Button` (click, Enter or Space sends `Activate`) with Tab navigation; hover lightens the fill, press darkens it and scales to 0.96 (OutQuint, 150 ms), and keyboard focus adds a 2 px white ring.
- `motion.rs`: `Ease::OutQuint` and `Ease::OutElastic`; `Tween<P>` drives `OffsetX`, `OffsetY`, `Scale` (`UiTransform`, so layout does not move), `Width` (`Node::width`) or `Opacity` (`UiOpacity`) and can be retargeted from wherever it is. `ReducedMotion` (`--reduced-motion`) makes every tween land at once.
- `backdrop.rs` (+ `backdrop.wgsl`): the one background behind every screen. Screens set the `Backdrop` resource (art handle, blur 0 to 1, dim from `backdrop::dim`: select 0.38, loading 0.55, gameplay 0.45, results 0.62) and never draw their own. A camera of its own (`BackdropCamera`, order -100) draws two full-window layers with `BackdropMaterial`; cameras over it use `ClearColorConfig::None`. `art_image` builds an image with its mip chain; the shader covers the window with the art (never stretched) and blurs by sampling the mip level that matches the radius (blur 1 is the mockups' 40 px at 1600 px wide), so the slider is smooth and cheap. A new art cross-fades over 400 ms (OutQuint; at once with reduced motion). `key_art_image` turns `iwbtg_format::key_art::resolve` into art (package key art, else the first ranked room's background, tiled over the room as the room shows it, or its colour, else the client's own dusk scene). `screen.rs` sets it from the package when the game opens.
- `icons.rs`: `Icon` (the stat marks, search, back, random, music bars) and `icon(Icon, height, colour)`. The mockups' SVG paths are rasterised with tiny-skia at the node's laid-out pixel size and tinted by the node's colour, so icons are crisp at any scale on native and wasm32. `widgets::stat` takes an optional icon.
- `notice.rs`: notices, short cards in the bottom left of the window that slide in and fade in (OutQuint, 400 ms), stay 6 s (real time, so a pause does not hold them) and fade out. `notice::post(Kind, message)` queues one from anywhere, any thread, even before the app exists (settings load first); the kit's `notice` system shows queued ones on the next frame. A `Kind` gives the heading (capitals, accent), the body (soft ink, wrapped, cut at 100 characters with `…`) and whether it shows once per session (`Shown`): storage problems show once (`storage::warn`, #566); a dropped file that is not a package (#648) is one more variant and one `post`. The card is the player card's colour without its glass (`tokens::CARD_SOLID`) with a 4 px accent band on the left and a drop shadow, over the HUD and the pause overlay (`GlobalZIndex(30)`). The HUD sets `notice::Area` to the leaderboard's column, so a notice stays off the game; its stack is that column less a 12 px margin each side, and each card leaves 12 px each side for its shear, so the card, its shear and its shadow stay inside the column at 16:9 and 4:3 (#668). The stack is 180 to 340 UI px wide: 180 is the narrowest leaderboard column outside a small window (`hud::layout::MIN_LEFT`, 204) less the margins, which a HUD test keeps true. Only a small window (960x540) has a narrower column; there the card reaches over the game's edge rather than wrap word by word. `--notice-preview` (hidden) posts a sample storage notice for screenshots.
- `gallery.rs`: `iwbtg-client --ui-gallery [--screenshot <png>] [--reduced-motion] [--window WxH] [--ratio W:H]` shows every token and component at the mockups' 1920×1080, scaled to fit the window (`UiScale`; `--window` sets an exact size in physical pixels, `--ratio` alone makes a 1600-wide window of that ratio, so screenshots come out at the size asked for), for comparing with the canvas: hover a game panel and it slides 25 px left, Pop and Fade run the OutElastic and OutQuint tweens, Tab shows the focus ring, M toggles reduced motion. Its panel art is drawn at run time. The wasm32 build opens the gallery directly ([dev-setup](../../dev-setup.md#ui-gallery-in-a-browser)).
