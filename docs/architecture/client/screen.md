# The client: Screen

Part of [the client](../client.md).

Screen (`screen.rs`, `ScreenPlugin`): `area` is the part of the window the chosen ratio allows (the largest centred rectangle of that ratio); `hud::layout::compute` places the game and the HUD columns in it (see HUD below), and `apply` clips the game camera to the game's rectangle (`Camera.viewport`, always) and runs it at one world unit per `scale` physical pixels, so the view fills the rectangle and nothing of the room shows beside it. The game is drawn with nearest sampling at any scale; with integer scale on (F7, `--integer-scale`) the scale is the largest whole step that fits. Views: `view::follow_view` runs every frame after the sim's tick. It keeps `RoomSize` equal to the sim's current room, reads view 0 (`enabled` and `visible`; else the whole room) into `ViewRect`, centres the game camera on that rectangle and turns it by `view_angle`. `screen.rs` scales the view (not the room) to its rectangle. Only view 0 is drawn (ports of other views are not). With `--interpolate` the view (position and angle) is blended between its place before the last tick and its place now, like the instances, and lands at once after a room change, a snap of more than 32 px, a resize or a turn of more than 45 degrees in one tick. OPEN: the direction of `view_angle` on screen is assumed counter-clockwise (the gravity-flip trace logs only the value; research#94). Outside the game the window shows the [UI backdrop](ui.md) (the game's key art, blurred and dimmed for gameplay, over the whole window), with a black panel the size of the view under the game. `[` and `]` blur the backdrop less and more in steps of 10% (shown in the window title; saved as `display.backdrop_blur`, read when the game opens; the settings screen's slider, #53, replaces the keys). There is no settings screen yet (#53), so the choices are flags and keys, each saved in the settings file (see Settings): `--fullscreen`, `--ratio native|4:3|16:9|16:10|21:9|32:9`, `--window WIDTHxHEIGHT` (physical pixels) and the keys F11 (fullscreen), F10 (next ratio) and F9 (next windowed size: 800x608, 1280x720, 1600x1216, 1920x1080, 2560x1440, 3200x2432). The window title shows the size, scale, ratio, UI scale and blur. A `--screenshot` run with `--window` lays out at one physical px per logical px, so its picture does not depend on the machine's display scaling. `demo-art.pkg` has a background to see the blur.
