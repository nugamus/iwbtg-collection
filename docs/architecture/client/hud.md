# The client: HUD

Part of [the client](../client.md).

HUD (`hud/`, `HudPlugin`; docs/product.md, In-game display; artboards `Hud169`, `Hud43`, `Hud219`, `HudSmall`): three columns, the live leaderboard left, the game centre, run info right. `hud::layout::compute` is pure (unit-tested at every artboard size) and works in logical px (the artboards' CSS px; physical px divided by the display scaling). The game fills the area's height unless the columns would get less than 204 px (left) and 180 px (right), then it shrinks (`Hud43`: 0.8x at 1024x768). A window of 960x540 or less in either direction is small: the columns keep 124 px each, the leaderboard shows rank and avatar plus your time, run info stacks under the timer and the input display hides. Spare room is split evenly; each column stops at 360 px (times the UI scale) and the rest is backdrop (`Hud219`). Density: full from 240 px per column (at the UI scale), else compact. The UI scale (75 to 150%, Ctrl+= and Ctrl+-, `--ui-scale`) scales only the panels (`UiScale`), never the game: when a column is too narrow for the panel at that scale the panel scale stops at what fits (`Layout::content`). While the [results](results.md) show, `Suspended` hides the columns and the game's shadow and leaves the UI scale to the results page. Shift+Tab hides the whole HUD and gives the game the whole area; the HUD always starts shown. The panels (`hud/panels.rs`) draw on a camera of their own (order 10, UI only) and are rebuilt only when their density or width changes; the leaderboard is 92% of its column, 7% from the game, run info 84%, 9% from it. A box shadow around the game (`0 0 60px` at 60% black) is drawn by the backdrop's camera, under the game. The left column is rebuilt also when the leaderboard's runs are reloaded (`Live::generation`). Run info (`hud/run_info.rs`, #37; `RunInfo.dc.html`): `read` fills the `RunInfo` resource every frame from the sim and `show` writes it into the panel; the panel is rebuilt only when its `Look` changes (ranking, mods, toggles, bound extra actions). From top: a `Paused · timer running` line while the [pause menu](pause.md) is open (`hud::Paused`, written by `pause.rs`); the mod row, only for an unranked run (the amber Unranked pill: Play original, or a mod that is not ranked) or when mods are on (24 px sheared pills with the codes); a plain ranked run shows nothing there. The timer is `Sim::ranked_ticks` plus the run's paused ticks (`hud::Paused::ticks`) as `m:ss` with the hundredths smaller and in the soft colour. Deaths is the death counter (`global.death`, so a run from a save starts with its deaths); Stage is `stages_done + 1` of the map's stage splits + 1; This stage counts deaths since the stage began and starts again at a new stage or a retry (`StageDeaths`). The input display draws the four arrows and Jump, Shoot, Restart from the frame the last tick took (`input::Collector::last`), lit white with an accent bar while held or pressed; Extra 1-4 appear when the player has bound them. Small windows stack the stats under the timer and hide the input display. Every element toggles on its own (`[hud.show]`, `--hud-show`, `--hud-hide` with comma-separated names); `leaderboard` hides the left panel. The hidden flag `--hud-preview-held right,jump` draws keys as held for screenshot checks.

Live leaderboard (`hud/leaderboard.rs`, #494; `Leaderboard.dc.html`, `LbStates.dc.html`, `Hud169Local.dc.html`): the Local scope, your own top runs on the board the run counts for (`<map>`, `ranked/NM` and so on) from the `Stats` book; Global (#484) will feed other players' runs into the same `Entry` list. `track` reads `RunInfo` every frame into `Live`: the runs are loaded when the map or board changes and on a retry (the time going back), so a clear joins the board from the next run on. Your time is the run info timer; your place is 1 plus the runs that took that many ticks or fewer (`place`: you drop below a run only once your time exceeds it, a tie keeps it ahead). The panel puts every run and your row on a track at slot × 47 px (44 px rows, 3 px apart) inside a list of up to 7 rows with a `FadeMask`; `animate` retargets the rows' `OffsetY` tweens when your place changes (450 ms OutQuint, `tokens::motion::REORDER`), pops your row from 20 px narrower (OutElastic), updates the ranks and scrolls the track to keep your row centred, pushed to the edge at either end (`scroll`). The fades follow the scroll: a row's height at an end where a whole row lies past it, none where no row does; rows two or more out of view are hidden. Rows: rank (Barlow Condensed 16/700), the 30 px no-avatar mark (a `Shape`, so the fade reaches it; profiles come later), the date (`3 Nov 2026`, in the player's time zone: `leaderboard::local_offset` asks `chrono`, which reads the OS zone natively and `js_sys::Date` on wasm32; `date_at` takes a fixed offset for tests) over the time for your runs on `ROW_PAST`, `Now` over your live time on `ROW_YOU`, 20 px wider, with its shadow; names do not wrap and end in `…` where they do not fit (`widgets::ellipsis_text`, #636; the artboard's ellipsis), times do not wrap and are cut at the row. At a stage clear the pace flash replaces your time for 3 s (`PACE_HOLD`) and fades back (300 ms): `−1.20 vs #2`, your time at that split minus that of the run directly above you (at #1, the run below), green for `−`, red for `+` (a tie shows `+0.00`); runs whose splits do not match the game's segments give no flash. No runs: only your row and `No clears yet — be the first`. Modes: live; frozen ([results](results.md), `Live::frozen`: no updates, your row named `This run` with its final time written once instead of following run info, and when #1 is out of view it is pinned on top over three dots); small windows draw rank and avatar only, your row with its time and the bare delta (the flash also drops `vs #n` when your row is under 200 px). Compact panels narrow the rank column to 28 px so the time fits and drop the year from the dates (`3 Nov`; `Entry::short`) so they fit too. The hidden flag `--hud-preview-leaderboard local|pace|top|empty|frozen` shows the artboard's sample runs (nine runs, you at 40:02.10, also on the timer) for screenshot checks.
