# The client: Visual mods

Part of [the client](../client.md).

The visual mods (`visual.rs`, `VisualPlugin`, #550; [product](../../product.md#mods)): Hitboxes (`--mods HB`) and No background (`--mods NB`), picked like every mod ([mods](mods.md)). Both change only the picture: they read the sim's state and never write it, so a tick computes the same, the replay plays back the same and every hash stays. Both are ranked mods in `iwbtg_sim::mods::MODS` (whether Hitboxes stays ranked is open, #554), so `ModSet::unranked_by` and the HUD's mod row treat them like Sudden Death.

- **Hitboxes.** Each frame `draw_hitboxes` draws one sprite per live instance that is the kid (`kid` or `kid_flipped` class, green) or a hazard (the `killer` class, or a `killer` kind that kills in its own collision, red), invisible ones too. The sprite is the instance's collision mask placed where the last tick left it, as the sim places it (`collide::instance_box` with the runner's collide rules) and pixel by pixel as the sim tests it (`collide::overlap::point_in`): a precise spike shows its triangle, a scaled or turned mask its placed shape. Solid pixels on the mask's edge are opaque, the inside is faint, so what is under it stays readable. Hitboxes are never interpolated (`--interpolate` moves sprites only). They sit at z 998, over instances, tiles and foreground layers, under the overlay. A mask's image is cached by its mask, scale, angle and offset, so a room of identical spikes builds one image; masks over 2^22 pixels are not drawn.
- **No background.** The plugin inserts `visual::PlainBackdrop` (grey `#3a3a3a`); [room art](art.md) then draws that colour in place of the room's background colour and leaves out the background layers that are not foregrounds. The backdrop around the game follows (`screen::plain_art`, #739): where it would show the room's background (the first ranked room's image or colour) it is the same grey; the package's own `[key_art]` and the client's scene (a package with no art at all) are not the room's background and stay. Tiles, instances and foreground layers stay. The results' heatmap still shows the room's own art (`art::still`).
